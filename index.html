Hi, welcome to my website!

I am a researcher and engineer with a Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering and an M.Sc. in Computational Science and Engineering from the Technical University of Munich (TUM), and nearly nine years of combined academic and industrial experience at TUM and BMW Group. In my last role at TUM, I also led the research group Test Fields and Simulators.

My research interests include transportation and mobility systems, logistics, fleet management, connected and autonomous vehicles, and mobility digital twins. My work combines mathematical optimization, agent-based simulation, demand forecasting, artificial intelligence, and machine learning to develop efficient and sustainable solutions for complex systems.
