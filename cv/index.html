Professional Summary

Research scientist with a Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering and an M.Sc. in Computational Science and Engineering from the Technical University of Munich (TUM). Experienced in optimization, multi-agent simulation, machine learning, and control, with applications in transportation systems. Previous experience includes academic and industrial research at TUM, BMW, and Infineon Technologies. Research interests include modelling of complex transportation systems, multi-agent systems, self-adaptive digital twins, and reinforcement learning.
